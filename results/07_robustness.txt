# 4. Deeper data audit
Same customer, 2 transactions at the same branch within 5 minutes (possible split orders): 3
Same customer at two DIFFERENT branches within 30 minutes (possible shared IDs): 23
Customer-days with visits to 2+ branches: 267 of 21,112
Products sold at all 3 branches: 45 of 45
Busiest single hour in the data: 51 transactions (2026-06-23 21:00); 99th percentile hour: 32 -> no extreme spikes
Customer IDs: 3,494 used out of the range C00001-C03500
Conclusion: no structural problems in the transaction records themselves. Split orders (3) are negligible; 23 cases of one ID at two branches within 30 minutes (0.1% of identified visits) suggest occasional shared IDs. NOTE: the broader validity of Customer IDs is tested separately in script 13, which finds no detectable persistence; ID-based conclusions are withdrawn there.
