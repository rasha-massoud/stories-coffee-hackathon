Extreme low days (P(sales <= observed) < 0.1%): 0 observed. Only 336 of 24,435 product-branch-days have enough expected demand for even zero sales to be flagged, and the expected number of flags by chance is ~0.17. So observing 0 says little: this screen has LOW power to detect shortages.
Note: units include 2-unit lines, so a unit-Poisson model under-predicts zero days; a model of basket incidence would expect more zeros, so the small excess of zero days may be a modelling artefact rather than a shortage signal.
Reading: this daily screen does not provide sufficient evidence to recommend inventory investment, but it also has limited ability to detect shortages, especially for slow-selling products. Availability remains unresolved without stock records or hourly availability data.
