# 1. The rules behind branch prices and costs (observed)
Garden cost = round(Harbour cost x 1.43, 2): 45 of 45 products
Uptown cost = round(Harbour cost x 0.96, 2): 45 of 45 products
Garden price = round(Harbour price / 1.05, 2): 45 of 45 products
Uptown price within 1 cent of Harbour price x 0.915: 36 of 45 products
Uptown price within 1.1 cents of Harbour price x 0.915: 42 of 45 products
Uptown price = round(GARDEN price x 0.96, 2): 45 of 45 products
Reading: four exact rules. Uptown's prices are NOT a fixed share of Harbour's (only approximately x0.915); they are exactly Garden's rounded prices x 0.96. So the price lists form a chain Harbour -> /1.05 -> Garden -> x0.96 -> Uptown, and Garden's ~4.8% discount carries over to Uptown. Garden's recorded prices are ~4.8% below Harbour's while its recorded unit costs follow the 43% uplift rule. Whether either was intended is not visible in transactions; whoever owns the cost sheet and the price list can say why.
Price Garden would need to earn Harbour's margin at its current costs: on average +50% vs today (i.e. ~+43% above Harbour) - not realistic, which is why the cost side must be fixed first.
