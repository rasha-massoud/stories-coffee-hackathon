Reading: the busiest 15 minutes carry noticeably more than a quarter of the busiest hour, so staffing to hourly averages understates peak load. Stories should compare these peak-15-minute counts with how many orders one barista and one till can actually serve in 15 minutes (not in this data) - that comparison, not the hourly average, says when queues start. The December figures assume growth continues, which is not established.
