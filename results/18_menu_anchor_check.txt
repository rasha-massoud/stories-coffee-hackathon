Upper bound at risk if the item were removed AND every one of its baskets were lost: Espresso $5,912/yr, Mint Tea $6,915/yr, English Breakfast Tea $5,014/yr. Lower bound if every buyer simply switched to another item: about $0 (or a gain if they switch to a higher-GP item).
Reading: compare 'total basket GP' with the average basket. Items whose baskets are as valuable as average, and are rarely bought alone, may be part of larger orders; removing them risks more than their own small GP. The data can't tell whether customers would switch or leave, so the action is a one-branch removal test, not a menu decision.
