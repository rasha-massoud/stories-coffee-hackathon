M1 average MAPE 9.3% vs strongest baseline 10.0% vs best tree model 10.7%.
Why the tree models lose: they cannot extrapolate the growth trend - every test month has more traffic than any training month, and a tree can only predict values it has seen. With +8.5%/month growth this is a structural failure, not a tuning problem. Complexity does not help here; we keep the simple, explainable M1.

Company GP/day in May-Jun: $1,158. Smallest detectable change in one calendar month (30 days, 5% test, 80% power): 4.1% = $48/day.
Expected effect of +5% at Garden (elasticity 0): $27/day = 2.4% of company GP/day -> NOT detectable directly.
Expected effect of +5% at Garden (elasticity -1, nobody switches): $13/day = 1.1% of company GP/day -> NOT detectable directly.
Conclusion: the company-level profit effect of a Garden price rise (~1-3% of company GP) is far below what one month can detect. Judging the pilot on company GP directly would almost always say 'no effect'. Design used instead (scripts 14-15): measure Garden's traffic vs the frozen M1 forecast, cross-check against Garden / (Harbour + Uptown), and decide with a one-sided bound. (Customer IDs cannot measure switching - script 13.)
